# haomachat (Number Lookup & Marking Guide)

> haomachat is a free phone number service toolkit for Chinese users: number attribution lookup, number marking self-check & clearance guidance, number authentication knowledge, and SIM card comparison. All marking clearance portals are aggregated from official platforms.

## Tools
- [Number Attribution Lookup](https://example.com/en/tools/attribution): Query carrier, region and number segment for any Chinese phone number
- [Number Marking Self-Check](https://example.com/en/tools/marking-check): Aggregated official query portals from CAICT and major platforms (360, Tencent, Baidu, Huawei)
- [Number Marking Clearance](https://example.com/en/tools/marking-clear): Official appeal portals and step-by-step tutorials per platform
- [SIM Card Comparison](https://example.com/en/tools/sim-cards): China Mobile/Unicom/Telecom plan comparison
